Privacy Policy for Switchback

Last updated 2026-10-13 · Published by Elevate Entertainment Enterprises

Switchback records where you drive so it can reveal a map of the places you have explored. Your exploration history is stored on your own device. We do not sell your data, and we do not record your location when a trip is not running.

Location

When you start a trip, the app records your GPS location until you end the trip, including while the screen is off. A notification shows for as long as recording is on. This is the only time a route is recorded.

The app also reads your location, without recording it, while Get Me Home is guiding you and when you ask it to — for example "Use current location" when setting home or choosing an area to download.

Your trips, routes and explored roads are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded.

Accounts and friends (optional)

Friend features need an account. If you create one, we store your email address, a password managed by Google Firebase Authentication, and the display name you choose. The core app works without an account.

With an account, the following is stored in our cloud database (Google Cloud Firestore): your list of friends; short-lived invite codes, which expire within 24 hours; and, for the friends leaderboard, the names of the areas you have explored (such as a county) and your completion percentage in each. Only your friends can see these.

If you turn on live sharing during a trip, your current position is sent to your friends while that trip runs. It is deleted when the trip ends or you turn sharing off.

Advertising

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob when you are not on a trip. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and information about ad interactions to serve and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first, and can change your choice under Settings › About › Ad privacy choices. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Removing ads by in-app purchase turns the ad service off entirely, which stops ad-related data collection.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened. We never receive or store your payment details.

Other services the app connects to

Downloading map data contacts GitHub, where our map files are published. Showing the map for an area you have not downloaded contacts OpenFreeMap, which provides map images. Checking for app updates contacts Expo. These services see your IP address as part of the request. No location history or personal information is sent to them.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your location with anyone except a friend you choose to share a live trip with. We do not record your location outside an active trip. We do not use your trips or location for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. You can export a full backup to a file at any time and import it on another device.

Account data stays in the cloud until you delete your account. You can delete your account and all of its cloud data in the app under Settings › Friends. You can also email us to ask for deletion and we will do it within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can use the app without an account. You can turn off location permission at any time in your device settings, though trips cannot be recorded without it. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data — contact us to use them.

Children

Switchback is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted in the app, and the date above will change.
